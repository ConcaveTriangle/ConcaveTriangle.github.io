Language models for legal education

An overview of AI-Assisted Legal Education for Student Grouping Using Large Language Models.

ACAIT 2024 · Pages 283–290

The question

How could a language model support legal learning and educational tasks such as grouping students?

The system

The paper introduces LawEducator, a conversational assistant combining a pre-trained language model, domain-specific fine-tuning, instructional alignment, and retrieval from legal texts. Confidence alone is a fairly poor citation.

The scope

The study discusses applications to student grouping and examines the benefits and limitations of using language models in legal education.

Publication details

Authors: Jiaxu Miao, Yiquan Wu, and Xinyi Guo.

Title: AI-Assisted Legal Education for Student Grouping Using Large Language Models.

Venue: 2024 8th Asian Conference on Artificial Intelligence Technology (ACAIT), pages 283–290.
